Google Play dimensions & content

This studio exports phone assets at 1080 × 1920 and feature graphics at 1024 × 500. Phone screenshot uploads permit dimensions from 320 to 3840 px, with the longer side no more than twice the shorter side. JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha is required.

Use at least two screenshots for a listing. For recommendation formats, Google asks for at least four app screenshots (three for games) in 9:16 or 16:9 at 1080 px minimum. Show the real app, keep taglines brief, avoid rankings, pricing claims and store badges. Previewing or exporting does not validate content or approve an upload.
